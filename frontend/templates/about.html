MODEL TRANSPARENCY

How InboxGuard analyzes email

A supervised email classifier plus inspectable secondary threat indicators. No link is fetched or opened.

TRAINING DATA

SpamAssassin Public Corpus

The email model is trained only on full email messages. The UCI SMS Spam Collection and its existing model remain separate; SMS results are not presented as email performance. The source corpus provides spam and legitimate labels, not verified phishing or promotional categories.

Phishing, suspicious, and promotional labels are secondary rule-assisted assessments—not supervised classes from this corpus.

METHOD

Signals and uncertainty

  • Subject, word-level and character-level TF-IDF, sender, and structural features.
  • Model spam score is calibrated only if calibration and holdout checks were completed.
  • Risk score is a separate transparent heuristic—not a probability of compromise.
  • Scores near the decision boundary are labeled uncertain for human review.
  • URL patterns are inspected locally; external URLs are never requested.
